# REPLACEMENT PART FOR AVO MK3 Knob Skirt

I designed replacement transparent knob skirts and ordered four pieces from
[JLC3DP](https://jlc3dp.com/), of which two were used.

## Manufacturing details

| Setting | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Technology | SLA resin printing |
| Material | 8001 Resin |
| Colour | Transparent |
| Surface finish | 01 Oil Spraying |
| Quantity | 4 |

As shown in `transparent_knob_skirt_1`, I added the white pointer mark using
white nail polish. The area was masked with Kapton tape, and any excess polish
was removed with isopropyl alcohol.

In `transparent_knob_skirt_2`, the original knob skirt is shown on the left
and the 3D-printed replacement on the right.

I am very pleased with the result, although only time will tell how well the
material holds up. The hole for the potentiometer shaft was slightly tight,
but in my case the fit was perfect.
